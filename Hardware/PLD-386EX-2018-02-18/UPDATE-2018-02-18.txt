Mandatory circuit update:  SBC-386EX v. 2.0 board

The following update is required by U22 GAL  DCTL16-7

1. connect U9.9 (74HCT14) to U22.9 (DCTL16 GAL)

After the update is installed, earlier versions of the DCTL16 are still operational.  However,
the dual Chip Select anomaly will still be encountered.  The update above, along with the GAL
update should solve the dual Chip Select problem.
